⚽ Football Value Capital

Seven-factor match analysis model combining current form, attacking power, defence, team quality, league level, venue strength and goal environment.

🏠 HOME 🎯 KEY MATCH FACTOR ✈️ AWAY
📈 FORM Recent results, momentum, consistency and current performance trend.
⚽ ATTACK Scoring ability, chance creation, finishing and attacking pressure.
🛡️ DEFENCE Defensive organization, goals conceded, chance prevention and resilience.
🏆 TEAM QUALITY Overall squad strength, player quality, depth, experience and tactical quality.
🌍 LEAGUE LEVEL Measures the competitive strength of the team's domestic league. This allows the model to distinguish situations such as a Premier League team facing a Championship team.
🏠 HOME / AWAY STRENGTH Home team's home performance versus away team's away performance.
🎯 GOAL ENVIRONMENT Expected scoring character of the matchup: open, balanced, controlled, high-event or low-event.
Model weighting:
Form: 15% | Attack: 18% | Defence: 16% | Team Quality: 17% | League Level: 12% | Home/Away Strength: 17% | Goal Environment: 5%

Team Quality and League Level influence the overall strength calculation, while Goal Environment is kept mainly for goal and match-character analysis rather than allowing it to dominate the winner prediction.
