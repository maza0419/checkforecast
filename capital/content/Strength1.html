🧠 HOW THE NUMBERS PRODUCED THE RATINGS

📈 FORM

Form is based primarily on the team's last 10 results.

Points = Wins × 3 + Draws × 1

The resulting Points Per Game (PPG) is converted to the rating scale.

⚽ ATTACK

Attack is determined mainly by goals scored per match.

More goals per match = higher attack rating.

The app compares the scoring rate against a football performance scale.

🛡️ DEFENCE

Defence uses goals conceded per match.

Lower goals conceded = better defence.

A team conceding fewer goals receives a higher defensive rating.

🏆 TEAM QUALITY

Team Quality combines:
• Results
• Points per game
• Goals scored
• Goals conceded
• Goal difference

It is broader than simple form.

🌍 LEAGUE LEVEL

League Level is not derived from one team's wins and goals.

It represents the strength of the competition. For example, a team's statistics should be interpreted within the level of the league where those statistics were produced.

🏠 HOME / AWAY STRENGTH

Home Strength uses the home team's home results.

Away Strength uses the away team's away results.

This prevents overall form from hiding venue-specific performance.

🎯 GOAL ENVIRONMENT

Goal Environment considers the scoring and conceding rates of both teams.

It asks:
How many total goals are these teams producing and allowing?
