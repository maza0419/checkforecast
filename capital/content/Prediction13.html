Recommended Interpretation

⚠️ This analyzer produces statistical signals from the data entered. It does not guarantee a particular match result or event. The quality of the output depends heavily on the quality and completeness of the input data.
Football Value Capital calculates Attack from the number of matches in which the team scored at least one goal. Defence is calculated from the number of matches in which the team kept a clean sheet. The percentages displayed are model scores and statistical signals, not guaranteed probabilities of future events.
