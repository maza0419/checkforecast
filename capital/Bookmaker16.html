Recommended Interpretation

⚠️ This system generates model-based statistical signals from the information entered. The scores are not guaranteed probabilities and cannot guarantee a match result.
Attack calculation: Matches the team scored in ÷ total matches × 100.

Defence calculation: Matches successfully defended / clean-sheet matches ÷ total matches × 100.

Therefore, Attack and Defence are based on match occurrence, not the number of goals.
